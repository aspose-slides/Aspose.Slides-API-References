## duration-Eigenschaft
Gets or sets the duration of the slide transition effect in milliseconds.
            Lesen/Schreiben **int**.


### Hinweise

Corresponds to the `p14:dur` attribute of the `p:transition` element in the PresentationML schema.
            If not set, the duration is determined automatically based on the [`ISlideShowTransition.speed`](/slides/python-net/de/aspose.slides/islideshowtransition/speed) property
            and the transition type.
