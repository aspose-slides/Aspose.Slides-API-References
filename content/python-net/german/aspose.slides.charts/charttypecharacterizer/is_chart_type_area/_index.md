## is_chart_type_area(chart_type) {#charttype}
Gibt true zurück, wenn chartType einer der Area-Untertypen ist.
Die Menge der Untertypen entspricht der jeweiligen Menge in PowerPoint (siehe „Change Chart Type“-Dialog in PowerPoint):
[`ChartType.AREA`](/slides/python-net/de/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/AREA),
[`ChartType.PERCENTS_STACKED_AREA`](/slides/python-net/de/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/PERCENTS_STACKED_AREA),
[`ChartType.PERCENTS_STACKED_AREA_3D`](/slides/python-net/de/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/PERCENTS_STACKED_AREA_3D),
[`ChartType.STACKED_AREA`](/slides/python-net/de/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/STACKED_AREA),
[`ChartType.STACKED_AREA_3D`](/slides/python-net/de/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/STACKED_AREA_3D),
[`ChartType.AREA_3D`](/slides/python-net/de/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/AREA_3D).
