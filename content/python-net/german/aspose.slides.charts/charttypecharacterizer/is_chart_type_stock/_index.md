## is_chart_type_stock(chart_type) {#charttype}
Gibt true zurück, wenn chartType einer der Stock-Untertypen ist.
            Die Menge der Untertypen entspricht der entsprechenden Menge in PowerPoint (siehe Dialog "Change Chart Type" in PowerPoint):
            [`ChartType.HIGH_LOW_CLOSE`](/slides/python-net/de/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/HIGH_LOW_CLOSE),
            [`ChartType.OPEN_HIGH_LOW_CLOSE`](/slides/python-net/de/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/OPEN_HIGH_LOW_CLOSE),
            [`ChartType.VOLUME_HIGH_LOW_CLOSE`](/slides/python-net/de/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/VOLUME_HIGH_LOW_CLOSE),
            [`ChartType.VOLUME_OPEN_HIGH_LOW_CLOSE`](/slides/python-net/de/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/VOLUME_OPEN_HIGH_LOW_CLOSE).
