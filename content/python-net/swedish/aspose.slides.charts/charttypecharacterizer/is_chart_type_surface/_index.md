## is_chart_type_surface(chart_type) {#charttype}

Undertypsuppsättning motsvarar den lämpliga uppsättningen i PowerPoint (se "Change Chart Type" dialog i PowerPoint):
[`ChartType.SURFACE_3D`](/slides/python-net/sv/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/SURFACE_3D),
[`ChartType.WIREFRAME_SURFACE_3D`](/slides/python-net/sv/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/WIREFRAME_SURFACE_3D),
[`ChartType.CONTOUR`](/slides/python-net/sv/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/CONTOUR),
