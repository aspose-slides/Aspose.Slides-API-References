## is_chart_type_stock(chart_type) {#charttype}
Returnerar true om chartType är en av Stock-undertyperna.
            Undertypsuppsättningen motsvarar den lämpliga uppsättningen i PowerPoint (se dialogrutan "Change Chart Type" i PowerPoint):
            [`ChartType.HIGH_LOW_CLOSE`](/slides/python-net/sv/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/HIGH_LOW_CLOSE),
            [`ChartType.OPEN_HIGH_LOW_CLOSE`](/slides/python-net/sv/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/OPEN_HIGH_LOW_CLOSE),
            [`ChartType.VOLUME_HIGH_LOW_CLOSE`](/slides/python-net/sv/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/VOLUME_HIGH_LOW_CLOSE),
            [`ChartType.VOLUME_OPEN_HIGH_LOW_CLOSE`](/slides/python-net/sv/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/VOLUME_OPEN_HIGH_LOW_CLOSE).
