## is_chart_type_scatter(chart_type) {#charttype}
Returnerar true om chartType är en av Scatter-undertyperna.
            Undertypsuppsättningen motsvarar den lämpliga uppsättningen i PowerPoint (se "Change Chart Type"-dialogen i PowerPoint):
            [`ChartType.SCATTER_WITH_MARKERS`](/slides/python-net/sv/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/SCATTER_WITH_MARKERS),
            [`ChartType.SCATTER_WITH_SMOOTH_LINES`](/slides/python-net/sv/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/SCATTER_WITH_SMOOTH_LINES),
            [`ChartType.SCATTER_WITH_SMOOTH_LINES_AND_MARKERS`](/slides/python-net/sv/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/SCATTER_WITH_SMOOTH_LINES_AND_MARKERS),
            [`ChartType.SCATTER_WITH_STRAIGHT_LINES`](/slides/python-net/sv/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/SCATTER_WITH_STRAIGHT_LINES),
            [`ChartType.SCATTER_WITH_STRAIGHT_LINES_AND_MARKERS`](/slides/python-net/sv/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/SCATTER_WITH_STRAIGHT_LINES_AND_MARKERS).
