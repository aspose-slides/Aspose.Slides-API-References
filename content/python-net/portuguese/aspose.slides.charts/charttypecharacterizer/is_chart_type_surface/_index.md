## is_chart_type_surface(chart_type) {#charttype}
Retorna true se chartType for um dos subtipos Surface.
            Conjunto de subtipos corresponde ao conjunto apropriado no PowerPoint (veja a caixa de diálogo "Change Chart Type" no PowerPoint):
            [`ChartType.SURFACE_3D`](/slides/python-net/pt/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/SURFACE_3D),
            [`ChartType.WIREFRAME_SURFACE_3D`](/slides/python-net/pt/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/WIREFRAME_SURFACE_3D),
            [`ChartType.CONTOUR`](/slides/python-net/pt/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/CONTOUR),
            [`ChartType.WIREFRAME_CONTOUR`](/slides/python-net/pt/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/WIREFRAME_CONTOUR).
