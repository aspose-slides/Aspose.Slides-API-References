## is_chart_type_stock(chart_type) {#charttype}
Retorna true se chartType for um dos subtipos de Stock.
            O conjunto de subtipos corresponde ao conjunto adequado no PowerPoint (veja a caixa de diálogo "Change Chart Type" no PowerPoint):
            [`ChartType.HIGH_LOW_CLOSE`](/slides/python-net/pt/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/HIGH_LOW_CLOSE),
            [`ChartType.OPEN_HIGH_LOW_CLOSE`](/slides/python-net/pt/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/OPEN_HIGH_LOW_CLOSE),
            [`ChartType.VOLUME_HIGH_LOW_CLOSE`](/slides/python-net/pt/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/VOLUME_HIGH_LOW_CLOSE),
            [`ChartType.VOLUME_OPEN_HIGH_LOW_CLOSE`](/slides/python-net/pt/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/VOLUME_OPEN_HIGH_LOW_CLOSE).
