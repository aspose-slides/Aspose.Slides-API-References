## is_chart_type_scatter(chart_type) {#charttype}
Retorna verdadeiro se chartType for um dos subtipos de Scatter.
            O conjunto de subtipos corresponde ao conjunto apropriado no PowerPoint (veja a caixa de diálogo "Change Chart Type" no PowerPoint):
            [`ChartType.SCATTER_WITH_MARKERS`](/slides/python-net/pt/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/SCATTER_WITH_MARKERS),
            [`ChartType.SCATTER_WITH_SMOOTH_LINES`](/slides/python-net/pt/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/SCATTER_WITH_SMOOTH_LINES),
            [`ChartType.SCATTER_WITH_SMOOTH_LINES_AND_MARKERS`](/slides/python-net/pt/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/SCATTER_WITH_SMOOTH_LINES_AND_MARKERS),
            [`ChartType.SCATTER_WITH_STRAIGHT_LINES`](/slides/python-net/pt/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/SCATTER_WITH_STRAIGHT_LINES),
            [`ChartType.SCATTER_WITH_STRAIGHT_LINES_AND_MARKERS`](/slides/python-net/pt/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/SCATTER_WITH_STRAIGHT_LINES_AND_MARKERS).
