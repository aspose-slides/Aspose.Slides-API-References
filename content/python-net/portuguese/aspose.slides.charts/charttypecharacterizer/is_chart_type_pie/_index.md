## is_chart_type_pie(chart_type) {#charttype}
Retorna true se chartType for um dos subtipos de Pizza.
            O conjunto de subtipos corresponde ao conjunto apropriado no PowerPoint (veja a caixa de diálogo "Change Chart Type" no PowerPoint):
            [`ChartType.BAR_OF_PIE`](/slides/python-net/pt/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/BAR_OF_PIE),
            [`ChartType.EXPLODED_PIE`](/slides/python-net/pt/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/EXPLODED_PIE),
            [`ChartType.EXPLODED_PIE_3D`](/slides/python-net/pt/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/EXPLODED_PIE_3D),
            [`ChartType.PIE`](/slides/python-net/pt/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/PIE),
            [`ChartType.PIE_3D`](/slides/python-net/pt/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/PIE_3D),
            [`ChartType.PIE_OF_PIE`](/slides/python-net/pt/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/PIE_OF_PIE).
