## is_chart_type_scatter(chart_type) {#charttype}
Zwraca true, jeśli chartType jest jednym z podtypów Scatter.
            Zestaw podtypów odpowiada odpowiedniemu zestawowi w programie PowerPoint (zobacz okno dialogowe „Change Chart Type” w programie PowerPoint):
            [`ChartType.SCATTER_WITH_MARKERS`](/slides/python-net/pl/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/SCATTER_WITH_MARKERS),
            [`ChartType.SCATTER_WITH_SMOOTH_LINES`](/slides/python-net/pl/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/SCATTER_WITH_SMOOTH_LINES),
            [`ChartType.SCATTER_WITH_SMOOTH_LINES_AND_MARKERS`](/slides/python-net/pl/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/SCATTER_WITH_SMOOTH_LINES_AND_MARKERS),
            [`ChartType.SCATTER_WITH_STRAIGHT_LINES`](/slides/python-net/pl/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/SCATTER_WITH_STRAIGHT_LINES),
            [`ChartType.SCATTER_WITH_STRAIGHT_LINES_AND_MARKERS`](/slides/python-net/pl/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/SCATTER_WITH_STRAIGHT_LINES_AND_MARKERS).
