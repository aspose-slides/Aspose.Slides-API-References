## IMotionEffect 類別

| [`from_`](/slides/python-net/zh-hant/aspose.slides.animation/imotioneffect/from_/) | 指定動畫開始的 x/y 座標（以百分比表示）。<br/>            Read/write [`PointF`](/slides/python-net/zh-hant/aspose.slides/pointf). |
