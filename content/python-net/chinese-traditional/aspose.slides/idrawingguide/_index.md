| [`orientation`](/slides/python-net/zh-hant/aspose.slides/idrawingguide/orientation/) | 回傳或設定繪圖參考線的方向。<br/>            可讀寫 [`Orientation`](/slides/python-net/zh-hant/aspose.slides/orientation). |
