## is_chart_type_scatter(chart_type) {#charttype}
如果 chartType 是 Scatter 子類型之一，返回 true。
            子類型集合對應 PowerPoint 中的相應集合（請參閱 PowerPoint 中的「變更圖表類型」對話方塊）：
            [`ChartType.SCATTER_WITH_MARKERS`](/slides/python-net/zh-hant/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/SCATTER_WITH_MARKERS),
            [`ChartType.SCATTER_WITH_SMOOTH_LINES`](/slides/python-net/zh-hant/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/SCATTER_WITH_SMOOTH_LINES),
            [`ChartType.SCATTER_WITH_SMOOTH_LINES_AND_MARKERS`](/slides/python-net/zh-hant/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/SCATTER_WITH_SMOOTH_LINES_AND_MARKERS),
            [`ChartType.SCATTER_WITH_STRAIGHT_LINES`](/slides/python-net/zh-hant/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/SCATTER_WITH_STRAIGHT_LINES),
            [`ChartType.SCATTER_WITH_STRAIGHT_LINES_AND_MARKERS`](/slides/python-net/zh-hant/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/SCATTER_WITH_STRAIGHT_LINES_AND_MARKERS).
