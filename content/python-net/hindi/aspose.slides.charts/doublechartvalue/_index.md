## DoubleChartValue क्लास

Represent double value which can be stored in pptx presentation document in two ways:
            1) in cell/cells of workbook related to chart;
            2) as literal value.

**विरासत:**[`DoubleChartValue`](/slides/python-net/hi/aspose.slides.charts/doublechartvalue) → [`BaseChartValue`](/slides/python-net/hi/aspose.slides.charts/basechartvalue)
