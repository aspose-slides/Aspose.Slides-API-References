## embedded_workbook_type プロパティ
埋め込みワークブックのタイプを取得します。
            [`WorkbookType.NOT_DEFINED`](/slides/python-net/ja/aspose.slides.charts/workbooktype/NOT_DEFINED) を返します、[`ChartData.data_source_type`](/slides/python-net/ja/aspose.slides.charts/chartdata/data_source_type) が 
            [`ChartDataSourceType.EXTERNAL_WORKBOOK`](/slides/python-net/ja/aspose.slides.charts/chartdatasourcetype/EXTERNAL_WORKBOOK).
            読み取り専用 [`WorkbookType`](/slides/python-net/ja/aspose.slides.charts/workbooktype)。
