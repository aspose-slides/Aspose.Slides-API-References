## Blur クラス

Represents a Blur effect that is applied to the entire shape, including its fill.
            All color channels, including alpha, are affected.

**継承:**[`Blur`](/slides/python-net/ja/aspose.slides.effects/blur) → [`ImageTransformOperation`](/slides/python-net/ja/aspose.slides.effects/imagetransformoperation) → [`PVIObject`](/slides/python-net/ja/aspose.slides/pviobject)
