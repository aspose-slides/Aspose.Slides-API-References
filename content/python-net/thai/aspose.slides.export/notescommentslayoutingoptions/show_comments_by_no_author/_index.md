## show_comments_by_no_author คุณสมบัติ
Gets or sets the visibility of comments that do not have an author. If true then comments 
             will be displayed.
             (Applies only if comments are displayed).


### หมายเหตุ

ค่าเริ่มต้นคือ **false** .
