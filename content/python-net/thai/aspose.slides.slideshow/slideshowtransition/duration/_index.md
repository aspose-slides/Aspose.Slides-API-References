## duration คุณสมบัติ
Gets or sets the duration of the slide transition effect in milliseconds.
            Read/write **int**.

### หมายเหตุ
Corresponds to the `p14:dur` attribute of the `p:transition` element in the PresentationML schema.
            If not set, the duration is determined automatically based on the [`SlideShowTransition.speed`](/slides/python-net/th/aspose.slides.slideshow/slideshowtransition/speed) property
            and the transition type.
