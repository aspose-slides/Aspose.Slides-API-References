## gap_width ویژگی
Gets or sets the space between bar or column clusters, as a percentage of the bar or column width.
            The value must be between 0 and 500.
            خواندنی/نوشتنی **int**.


### توضیحات

این ویژگی برای مجموعه‌ای از انواع زیر اعمال می‌شود:
            زیرگونه‌های نوار (see also **Aspose.Slides.Charts.ChartTypeCharacterizer.IsChartTypeBar(Aspose.Slides.Chart** method), 
            زیرگونه‌های ستون (see also **Aspose.Slides.Charts.ChartTypeCharacterizer.IsChartTypeColumn(Aspose.Slides.Chart** method), 
            [`ChartType.BAR_OF_PIE`](/slides/python-net/fa/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/BAR_OF_PIE),
            [`ChartType.PIE_OF_PIE`](/slides/python-net/fa/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/PIE_OF_PIE),
            [`ChartType.BOX_AND_WHISKER`](/slides/python-net/fa/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/BOX_AND_WHISKER),
            [`ChartType.FUNNEL`](/slides/python-net/fa/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/FUNNEL),
