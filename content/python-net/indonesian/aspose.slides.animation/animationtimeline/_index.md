## AnimationTimeLine kelas

Mewakili timeline animasi.

Tipe AnimationTimeLine mengekspos anggota berikut:

## Properti

| Properti | Deskripsi |
| :- | :- |
| [`interactive_sequences`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides.animation/animationtimeline/interactive_sequences/) | Mengembalikan koleksi urutan interaktif.<br/>            Urutan ini hanya dapat berisi efek dengan "klik pada bentuk" yang menentukan bentuk target.<br/>            Hanya-baca [`ISequenceCollection`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides.animation/isequencecollection). |
| [`main_sequence`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides.animation/animationtimeline/main_sequence/) | Mengembalikan urutan utama yang hanya dapat berisi koleksi efek utama.<br/>            Hanya-baca [`ISequence`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides.animation/isequence). |
| [`text_animation_collection`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides.animation/animationtimeline/text_animation_collection/) | Mengembalikan koleksi animasi teks.<br/>            Hanya-baca [`ITextAnimationCollection`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides.animation/itextanimationcollection). |
