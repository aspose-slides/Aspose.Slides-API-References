## IPresentationAnimationPlayer kelas

Mewakili pemutar animasi.
            
            Animasi yang dihasilkan oleh [`PresentationAnimationsGenerator`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides.export/presentationanimationsgenerator) melalui peristiwa **NewAnimation**.

Tipe IPresentationAnimationPlayer mengekspos anggota berikut:

## Properti

| Properti | Deskripsi |
| :- | :- |
| [`duration`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides.export/ipresentationanimationplayer/duration/) | Dapatkan durasi animasi [ms] |

## Metode

| Metode | Deskripsi |
| :- | :- |
| [`set_time_position(self, time)`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides.export/ipresentationanimationplayer/set_time_position/#float) | Atur posisi waktu animasi dalam [`IPresentationAnimationPlayer.duration`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides.export/ipresentationanimationplayer/duration). |
| [`get_frame(self)`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides.export/ipresentationanimationplayer/get_frame/#) | Dapatkan frame untuk posisi waktu saat ini yang sebelumnya diatur dengan metode **Aspose.Slides.Export.IPresentationAnimationPlayer.SetTimePosition(Syste**. |
