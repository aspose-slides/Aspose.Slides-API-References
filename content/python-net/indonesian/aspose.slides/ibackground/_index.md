## IBackground kelas

Tipe IBackground mengekspos anggota-anggota berikut:

| Properti | Deskripsi |
| :- | :- |
| [`type`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides/ibackground/type/) | Mengembalikan jenis pengisian latar belakang.<br/>            Baca/tulis [`BackgroundType`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides/backgroundtype). |
