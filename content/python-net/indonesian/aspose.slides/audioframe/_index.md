## AudioFrame kelas

Mewakili klip audio pada slide.

**Warisan:**[`AudioFrame`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides/audioframe) → [`PictureFrame`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides/pictureframe) → [`GeometryShape`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides/geometryshape) → [`Shape`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides/shape)

Tipe AudioFrame mengekspos anggota berikut:
