## IMasterSlideHeaderFooterManager kelas

Mewakili manajer yang menyimpan perilaku placeholder footer slide master, tanggal-waktu, nomor halaman, dan semua placeholder anak.  
Child placeholders mean placeholders are contained on depending layout slides and depending slides.  
Depending layout slides and slides use and depend on master slide.
