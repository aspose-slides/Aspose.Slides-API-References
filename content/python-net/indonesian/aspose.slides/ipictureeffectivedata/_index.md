## IPictureEffectiveData kelas

Objek tak dapat diubah yang berisi properti gambar efektif.

Tipe IPictureEffectiveData mengekspos anggota-anggota berikut:

## Properti

| Properti | Deskripsi |
| :- | :- |
| [`image`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides/ipictureeffectivedata/image/) | Mengembalikan gambar tersemat.<br/>            Hanya baca [`IPPImage`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides/ippimage). |
| [`link_path_long`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides/ipictureeffectivedata/link_path_long/) | Mengembalikan URL gambar yang ditautkan.<br/>            Hanya baca **str**. |
| [`image_transform`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides/ipictureeffectivedata/image_transform/) | Mengembalikan koleksi efek transformasi gambar.<br/>            Hanya baca [`IImageTransformOCollectionEffectiveData`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides.effects/iimagetransformocollectioneffectivedata). |


### Catatan

Antarmuka ini digunakan sebagai bagian dari [`IPictureFillFormatEffectiveData`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides/ipicturefillformateffectivedata) dan [`IBulletFormatEffectiveData`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides/ibulletformateffectivedata).
