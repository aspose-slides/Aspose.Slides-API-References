## SummaryZoomFrame kelas

**Warisan:**[`SummaryZoomFrame`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides/summaryzoomframe) → [`GraphicalObject`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides/graphicalobject) → [`Shape`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides/shape)
