## delete_embedded_binary_objects properti
Menentukan apakah Aspose.Slides akan menghapus semua objek biner yang disematkan saat memuat presentasi.

Jenis-jenis objek biner yang disematkan:

* Proyek VBA [`IPresentation.vba_project`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides/ipresentation/vba_project)
* Data tertanam OLE Object [`IOleEmbeddedDataInfo.embedded_file_data`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides/ioleembeddeddatainfo/embedded_file_data)
* Data biner ActiveX Control [`IControl.active_x_control_binary`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides/icontrol/active_x_control_binary)

Baca/tulis **bool**.

### Keterangan

Nilai default adalah **false** .
