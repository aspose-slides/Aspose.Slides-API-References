## SummaryZoomSection kelas

Mewakili objek Summary Zoom Section dalam kerangka Summary Zoom.

**Warisan:**[`SummaryZoomSection`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides/summaryzoomsection) → [`SectionZoomFrame`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides/sectionzoomframe) → [`ZoomObject`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides/zoomobject) → [`GraphicalObject`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides/graphicalobject) → [`Shape`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides/shape)
