## Placeholder kelas

Mewakili placeholder pada slide.

Tipe Placeholder menampilkan anggota berikut:

## Properti

| Properti | Deskripsi |
| :- | :- |
| [`orientation`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides/placeholder/orientation/) | Mendapatkan atau menetapkan orientasi placeholder.<br/>            Hanya-baca [`Orientation`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides/orientation). |
| [`size`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides/placeholder/size/) | Mendapatkan atau menetapkan ukuran placeholder.<br/>            Hanya-baca [`PlaceholderSize`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides/placeholdersize). |
| [`type`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides/placeholder/type/) | Mendapatkan atau menetapkan tipe placeholder.<br/>            Hanya-baca [`PlaceholderType`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides/placeholdertype). |
| [`index`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides/placeholder/index/) | Mendapatkan atau menetapkan indeks placeholder.<br/>            Hanya-baca **int**. |
