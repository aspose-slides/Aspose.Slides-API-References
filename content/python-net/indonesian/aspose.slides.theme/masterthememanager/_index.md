## MasterThemeManager kelas

Menyediakan akses ke tema master presentasi.

**Pewarisan:**[`MasterThemeManager`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides.theme/masterthememanager) → [`BaseThemeManager`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides.theme/basethememanager)

## Properti

| Properti | Deskripsi |
| :- | :- |
| [`override_theme`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides.theme/masterthememanager/override_theme/) | Mengembalikan objek tema yang menimpa.<br/>            Baca/tulis [`IMasterTheme`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides.theme/imastertheme). |
| [`is_override_theme_enabled`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides.theme/masterthememanager/is_override_theme_enabled/) | Menentukan apakah OverrideTheme menimpa tema efektif yang diwariskan (Presentation.MasterTheme) atau tidak.<br/>            Baca/tulis **bool**. |
