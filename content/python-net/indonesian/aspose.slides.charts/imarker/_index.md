## IMarker kelas

Mewakili penanda dari sebuah chert.

Tipe IMarker mengekspos anggota-anggota berikut:

## Properti

| Properti | Deskripsi |
| :- | :- |
| [`symbol`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides.charts/imarker/symbol/) | Mewakili gaya penanda dalam diagram garis, diagram sebar, atau diagram radar.<br/>            Baca/tulis [`MarkerStyleType`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides.charts/markerstyletype). |
| [`format`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides.charts/imarker/format/) | Mendapatkan isi penanda.<br/>            Hanya-baca [`IFormat`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides.charts/iformat). |
| [`size`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides.charts/imarker/size/) | Mewakili ukuran penanda dalam diagram garis, diagram sebar, atau diagram radar.<br/>            Baca/tulis **int**. |
