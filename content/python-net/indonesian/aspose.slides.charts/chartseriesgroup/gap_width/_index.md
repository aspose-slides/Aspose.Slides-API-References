## gap_width properti
Mendapatkan atau mengatur ruang antara gugusan batang atau kolom, sebagai persentase lebar batang atau kolom.
            Nilai harus berada di antara 0 dan 500.
            Baca/tulis **int**.


### Keterangan

Properti ini berlaku untuk seri dengan tipe berikut:
            Bar subtipe (see also **Aspose.Slides.Charts.ChartTypeCharacterizer.IsChartTypeBar(Aspose.Slides.Chart** method), 
            Column subtipe (see also **Aspose.Slides.Charts.ChartTypeCharacterizer.IsChartTypeColumn(Aspose.Slides.Chart** method), 
            [`ChartType.BAR_OF_PIE`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/BAR_OF_PIE),
            [`ChartType.PIE_OF_PIE`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/PIE_OF_PIE),
            [`ChartType.BOX_AND_WHISKER`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/BOX_AND_WHISKER),
            [`ChartType.FUNNEL`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/FUNNEL),
            [`ChartType.HISTOGRAM`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/HISTOGRAM),
            [`ChartType.WATERFALL`](/slides/python-net/id/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/WATERFALL).
