## trend_lines proprietà
Raccolta di linee di tendenza delle serie.
            Solo lettura [`ITrendlineCollection`](/slides/python-net/it/aspose.slides.charts/itrendlinecollection).

### Osservazioni
TrendLines are available (not None) for data series in unstacked 2-D area, bar, column, line, 
            stock, xy (scatter), and bubble charts. A trendline are not available for data series 
            in any chart type that is stacked or 3-D. Trendlines are also not available for radar, 
            pie, surface, or doughnut charts.
