## gap_width 속성
Gets or sets the space between bar or column clusters, as a percentage of the bar or column width.
            값은 0과 500 사이여야 합니다.
            읽기/쓰기 **int**.


### 비고

This property applies to series of the following types:
            Bar 하위 유형 (또한 보기 **Aspose.Slides.Charts.ChartTypeCharacterizer.IsChartTypeBar(Aspose.Slides.Chart** method), 
            Column 하위 유형 (또한 보기 **Aspose.Slides.Charts.ChartTypeCharacterizer.IsChartTypeColumn(Aspose.Slides.Chart** method), 
            [`ChartType.BAR_OF_PIE`](/slides/python-net/ko/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/BAR_OF_PIE),
            [`ChartType.PIE_OF_PIE`](/slides/python-net/ko/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/PIE_OF_PIE),
            [`ChartType.BOX_AND_WHISKER`](/slides/python-net/ko/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/BOX_AND_WHISKER),
            [`ChartType.FUNNEL`](/slides/python-net/ko/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/FUNNEL),
            [`ChartType.HISTOGRAM`](/slides/python-net/ko/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/HISTOGRAM),
            [`ChartType.WATERFALL`](/slides/python-net/ko/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/WATERFALL).
