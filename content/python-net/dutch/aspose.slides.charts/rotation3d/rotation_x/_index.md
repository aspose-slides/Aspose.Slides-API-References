## rotation_x eigenschap
Returns or sets the rotation degree around the X-as, i.e. in de Y-richting for 3D-diagrammen (between -90 and 90 degrees).
            The property matches with the 21.2.2.157 rotX (X Rotation) item in ECMA-376 and with the "Y Rotation" option in PowerPoint 2007+.
            Lezen/schrijven **int**.
