## duration vlastnost
Gets or sets the duration of the slide transition effect in milliseconds.
            Číst/Zapisovat **int**.


### Poznámky

Corresponds to the `p14:dur` attribute of the `p:transition` element in the PresentationML schema.
            If not set, the duration is determined automatically based on the [`SlideShowTransition.speed`](/slides/python-net/cs/aspose.slides.slideshow/slideshowtransition/speed) vlastnost and the transition type.
