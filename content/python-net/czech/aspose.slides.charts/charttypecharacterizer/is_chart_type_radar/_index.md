## is_chart_type_radar(chart_type) {#charttype}

            [`ChartType.FILLED_RADAR`](/slides/python-net/cs/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/FILLED_RADAR),
            [`ChartType.RADAR`](/slides/python-net/cs/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/RADAR),
