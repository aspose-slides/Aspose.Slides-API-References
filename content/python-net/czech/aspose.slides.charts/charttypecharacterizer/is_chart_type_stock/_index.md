## is_chart_type_stock(chart_type) {#charttype}
Vrátí true, pokud je chartType jedním z podtypů Stock.
            Množina podtypů odpovídá příslušné množině v PowerPointu (viz "Change Chart Type" dialog v PowerPointu):
            [`ChartType.HIGH_LOW_CLOSE`](/slides/python-net/cs/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/HIGH_LOW_CLOSE),
            [`ChartType.OPEN_HIGH_LOW_CLOSE`](/slides/python-net/cs/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/OPEN_HIGH_LOW_CLOSE),
            [`ChartType.VOLUME_HIGH_LOW_CLOSE`](/slides/python-net/cs/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/VOLUME_HIGH_LOW_CLOSE),
            [`ChartType.VOLUME_OPEN_HIGH_LOW_CLOSE`](/slides/python-net/cs/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/VOLUME_OPEN_HIGH_LOW_CLOSE).
