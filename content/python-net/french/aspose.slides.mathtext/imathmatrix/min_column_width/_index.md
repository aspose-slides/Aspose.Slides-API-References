## min_column_width propriété
Largeur minimale de colonne en twips (1/20e d'un point)
            The gap spacing (also referred to as “Ecart de colonne” or “Largeur d'écart”) is added to 
            the MinColumnWidth to determine the total Espacement des colonnes de la matrice
            (distance between the same edges of different columns).
            Default: 0.
