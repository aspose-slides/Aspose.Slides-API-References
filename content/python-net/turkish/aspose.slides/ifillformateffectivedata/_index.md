## IFillFormatEffectiveData sınıfı

| Özellik | Açıklama |
| :- | :- |

| [`solid_fill_color`](/slides/python-net/tr/aspose.slides/ifillformateffectivedata/solid_fill_color/) | Dolgu rengini döndürür.<br/>            Salt okunur [`Color`](/slides/python-net/tr/aspose.slides/color). |
