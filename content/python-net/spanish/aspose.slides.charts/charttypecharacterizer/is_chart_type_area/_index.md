## is_chart_type_area(chart_type) {#charttype}
Devuelve true si chartType es uno de los subtipos de Area.
            El conjunto de subtipos corresponde al conjunto apropiado en PowerPoint (ver el cuadro de diálogo "Change Chart Type" en PowerPoint):
            [`ChartType.AREA`](/slides/python-net/es/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/AREA),
            [`ChartType.PERCENTS_STACKED_AREA`](/slides/python-net/es/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/PERCENTS_STACKED_AREA),
            [`ChartType.PERCENTS_STACKED_AREA_3D`](/slides/python-net/es/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/PERCENTS_STACKED_AREA_3D),
            [`ChartType.STACKED_AREA`](/slides/python-net/es/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/STACKED_AREA),
            [`ChartType.STACKED_AREA_3D`](/slides/python-net/es/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/STACKED_AREA_3D),
            [`ChartType.AREA_3D`](/slides/python-net/es/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/AREA_3D).
