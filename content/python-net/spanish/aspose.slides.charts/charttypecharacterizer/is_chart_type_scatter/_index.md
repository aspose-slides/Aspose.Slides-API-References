## is_chart_type_scatter(chart_type) {#charttype}
Devuelve true si chartType es uno de los subtipos Scatter.
            El conjunto de subtipos corresponde al conjunto apropiado en PowerPoint (ver el cuadro de diálogo "Change Chart Type" en PowerPoint):
            [`ChartType.SCATTER_WITH_MARKERS`](/slides/python-net/es/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/SCATTER_WITH_MARKERS),
            [`ChartType.SCATTER_WITH_SMOOTH_LINES`](/slides/python-net/es/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/SCATTER_WITH_SMOOTH_LINES),
            [`ChartType.SCATTER_WITH_SMOOTH_LINES_AND_MARKERS`](/slides/python-net/es/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/SCATTER_WITH_SMOOTH_LINES_AND_MARKERS),
            [`ChartType.SCATTER_WITH_STRAIGHT_LINES`](/slides/python-net/es/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/SCATTER_WITH_STRAIGHT_LINES),
            [`ChartType.SCATTER_WITH_STRAIGHT_LINES_AND_MARKERS`](/slides/python-net/es/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/SCATTER_WITH_STRAIGHT_LINES_AND_MARKERS).
