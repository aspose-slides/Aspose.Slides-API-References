## propiedad text_format
Devuelve el formato de texto del gráfico.
            La propiedad no se aplica a los siguientes tipos: [`ChartType.TREEMAP`](/slides/python-net/es/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/TREEMAP), [`ChartType.SUNBURST`](/slides/python-net/es/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/SUNBURST),
            [`ChartType.WATERFALL`](/slides/python-net/es/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/WATERFALL), [`ChartType.HISTOGRAM`](/slides/python-net/es/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/HISTOGRAM), [`ChartType.FUNNEL`](/slides/python-net/es/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/FUNNEL),[`ChartType.BOX_AND_WHISKER`](/slides/python-net/es/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/BOX_AND_WHISKER).
            Solo lectura [`IChartTextFormat`](/slides/python-net/es/aspose.slides.charts/icharttextformat).
