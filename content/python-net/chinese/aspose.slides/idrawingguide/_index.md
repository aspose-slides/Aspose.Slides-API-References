| [`orientation`](/slides/python-net/zh/aspose.slides/idrawingguide/orientation/) | 返回或设置绘图参考线的方向。<br/>            读/写 [`Orientation`](/slides/python-net/zh/aspose.slides/orientation). |
| [`position`](/slides/python-net/zh/aspose.slides/idrawingguide/position/) | 返回或设置绘图参考线相对于幻灯片左上角的点位置。<br/>            读/写 **float**. |
