## is_chart_type_pie(chart_type) {#charttype}
如果 chartType 是饼图的子类型之一，则返回 true。
            子类型集合对应于 PowerPoint 中的相应集合（参见 PowerPoint 中的 “Change Chart Type” 对话框）：
            [`ChartType.BAR_OF_PIE`](/slides/python-net/zh/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/BAR_OF_PIE),
            [`ChartType.EXPLODED_PIE`](/slides/python-net/zh/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/EXPLODED_PIE),
            [`ChartType.EXPLODED_PIE_3D`](/slides/python-net/zh/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/EXPLODED_PIE_3D),
            [`ChartType.PIE`](/slides/python-net/zh/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/PIE),
            [`ChartType.PIE_3D`](/slides/python-net/zh/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/PIE_3D),
            [`ChartType.PIE_OF_PIE`](/slides/python-net/zh/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/PIE_OF_PIE).
