## is_chart_type_stock(chart_type) {#charttype}
如果 chartType 是 Stock 子类型之一，则返回 true。
            子类型集合对应于 PowerPoint 中的相应集合（参见 PowerPoint 中的 “Change Chart Type” 对话框）：
            [`ChartType.HIGH_LOW_CLOSE`](/slides/python-net/zh/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/HIGH_LOW_CLOSE),
            [`ChartType.OPEN_HIGH_LOW_CLOSE`](/slides/python-net/zh/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/OPEN_HIGH_LOW_CLOSE),
            [`ChartType.VOLUME_HIGH_LOW_CLOSE`](/slides/python-net/zh/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/VOLUME_HIGH_LOW_CLOSE),
            [`ChartType.VOLUME_OPEN_HIGH_LOW_CLOSE`](/slides/python-net/zh/aspose.slides.charts/charttype/VOLUME_OPEN_HIGH_LOW_CLOSE).
