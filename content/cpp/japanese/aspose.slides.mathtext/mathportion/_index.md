## 備考

例:
```cpp
auto pres = System::MakeObject<Presentation>();

auto paragraph = shape->get_TextFrame()->get_Paragraphs()->idx_get(0);
auto mathPortion = System::MakeObject<MathPortion>();
paragraph->get_Portions()->Add(mathPortion);
```
