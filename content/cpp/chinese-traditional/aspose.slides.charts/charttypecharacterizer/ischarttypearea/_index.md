## ChartTypeCharacterizer::IsChartTypeArea(ChartType) 方法

如果 chartType 是 Area 子類型之一，則返回 true。子類型集合對應於 PowerPoint 中的相應集合（see \"Change Chart Type\" dialog in PowerPoint）：[ChartType::Area](../../charttype/), [ChartType::PercentsStackedArea](../../charttype/), [ChartType::PercentsStackedArea3D](../../charttype/), [ChartType::StackedArea](../../charttype/), [ChartType::StackedArea3D](../../charttype/), [ChartType::Area3D](../../charttype/).
