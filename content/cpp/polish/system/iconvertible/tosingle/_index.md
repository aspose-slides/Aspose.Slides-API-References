## IConvertible::ToSingle(System::SharedPtr\<System::IFormatProvider\>) metoda

```cpp
virtual float System::IConvertible::ToSingle(System::SharedPtr<System::IFormatProvider> provider)=0
```

### Argumenty

| Parametr | Typ | Opis |

### Wartość zwracana
