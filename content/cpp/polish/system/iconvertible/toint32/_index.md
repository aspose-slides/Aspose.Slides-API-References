## IConvertible::ToInt32(System::SharedPtr\<System::IFormatProvider\>) metoda

```cpp
virtual int32_t System::IConvertible::ToInt32(System::SharedPtr<System::IFormatProvider> provider)=0
```

### Argumenty

| Parametr | Typ | Opis |

### Wartość zwracana
