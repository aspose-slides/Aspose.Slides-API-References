## IHyperlinkQueries::GetHyperlinkMouseOvers() método

virtual System::SharedPtr<System::Collections::Generic::IList<System::SharedPtr<IHyperlinkContainer>>> Aspose::Slides::IHyperlinkQueries::GetHyperlinkMouseOvers()=0
