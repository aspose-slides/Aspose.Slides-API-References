## IPictureFillFormat::get_StretchOffsetLeft() طريقة

Returns left edge of the fill rectangle that is defined by a percentage offset from the left edge of the shape's bounding box. A positive percentage specifies an inset, while a negative percentage specifies an outset. قراءة **float**.

virtual float Aspose::Slides::IPictureFillFormat::get_StretchOffsetLeft()=0
