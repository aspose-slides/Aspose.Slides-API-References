## LayoutTargetType enumerasi

Jika tata letak area plot ditentukan secara manual, properti ini menentukan apakah menata area plot di dalamnya (tidak termasuk axis dan label axis) atau di luar (termasuk axis dan label axis).

```csharp
public enum LayoutTargetType
```

### Nilai

| Nama | Nilai | Deskripsi |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inner | `0` | Specifies that the plot area size shall determine the size of the plot area, not including the tick marks and axis labels. |
| Outer | `1` | Specifies that the plot area size shall determine the size of the plot area, the tick marks, and the axis labels. |
